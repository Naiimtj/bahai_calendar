Bahá'í Calendar — Privacy Policy

Data Collection

Bahá'í Calendar does not collect, store, or share any personal information. No user account is required.

Local Storage

Your preferences (language, theme, calendar view) are saved locally on your device using localStorage. This data never leaves your device.

API Calls

Bahá'í calendar dates are calculated entirely on your device. The app only contacts external services for sunrise/sunset times and location search; no personal data is sent in these requests.

Third Parties

No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the App Store listing.
