Bahá'í Calendar — Privacy Policy

Data Collection

Bahá'í Calendar has no user accounts and does not collect, sell, or share personal information with the developer. Your events, calendars, and reminders stay on your device.

Local Storage

Your preferences (language, theme, calendar view, saved location), the events you save in the app and the data shown in home-screen widgets are stored only on your device. They remain there until you delete them; deleting the app removes them all. Events and reminders you save to your device calendars stay in those apps. The developer keeps no copy, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

API Calls

Bahá'í dates are calculated entirely on your device. To show sunrise and sunset times, the app sends the coordinates of your chosen location to sunrise-sunset.org. When you tap “My location”, your city name is looked up with Apple Maps on iOS; on other platforms the coordinates are sent to BigDataCloud, which may also use them to improve its location services. When you search for a place, the text you type is sent to Open-Meteo, to Photon (OpenStreetMap) outside iOS, or to Apple Maps on iOS, and map images may be loaded from OpenStreetMap. Calendar subscriptions you add are downloaded from the address you enter. These requests are not linked to any identity or account, although the providers may see your IP address.

Third Parties

These services are used only to provide the features above and receive only the data each request needs. We only use providers that protect that data at least as well as this policy describes. No analytics, advertising, or tracking services are used, and the app does not track you.

Permissions

Location is requested only when you tap the location button, to find sunrise and sunset times. Calendar and Reminders access lets you see and create your own events next to Bahá'í dates; that data is read on your device and is never sent to the developer. Notifications are used for local alarms and reminders. You can revoke any permission in Settings at any time.
